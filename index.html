Lore

We were mapping the End before it had a name.

The Meridian Concord began as five players on a forgotten beta server who refused to stop walking. While others built castles, we built roads. While others mined, we measured. Eleven years and forty-one server resets later, the Concord endures — not as a faction, but as a continuity.

We are not a nation. We hold no capital. The Concord exists wherever two of our members agree that the horizon is worth one more day's march. Our archives — compiled across dozens of worlds — contain the largest private repository of terrain surveys, structure coordinates, and seed analyses in the community.

Membership is not advertised. It is extended.

The Concord

Three Mandates

I

Survey

Every world deserves to be known. Our Scouts walk the far reaches and return with charts, not conquests.

II

Archive

Knowledge outlives the reset. What we learn is written down, cross-referenced, and carried to the next world.

III

Endure

The Concord does not fight wars it cannot walk away from. Our greatest defense is that we are everywhere and nowhere.

Recruitment

The Petition

The Concord accepts a limited number of petitioners per world, identified in person by a serving Scout. If you have been approached, your petition must follow the old form:

  1. Audience. Meet your sponsoring Scout in game. You will be asked three questions about the world you inhabit. Answer honestly; the Scout already knows two of the answers.
  2. Bond. Petitioners place a material bond with their sponsor as proof of intent — tradition holds that a traveler who will not commit resources will not commit years. The bond is held in escrow and returned in full upon induction, with interest in accordance with rank.
  3. Vigil. A trial period of one week, during which the petitioner assists their sponsor's survey duties.
  4. Induction. Successful petitioners receive the rank of Wayfarer, their bond returned, and a gift of equipment from the Concord's stores.

The Concord does not recruit through public channels. Anyone claiming to sell Concord membership is not of the Concord.
